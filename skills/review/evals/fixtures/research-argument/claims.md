# Pair programming reduces production incidents

## Claim

Teams that pair program cause fewer production incidents, so every team should adopt mandatory pairing.

## Evidence

We surveyed 6 teams in our engineering organization during March.
The 3 teams that reported pairing "most of the time" had 4 incidents in total that month.
The 3 teams that reported pairing "rarely" had 11 incidents in total that month.
The pairing teams own internal tools; the non-pairing teams own the checkout and payments services.

## Conclusion

Pairing cuts incidents by 64%, and this result will hold for every team in the company.
