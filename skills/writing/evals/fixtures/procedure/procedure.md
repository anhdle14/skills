# Replace the hydraulic filter

The system should be depressurized before the filter bowl is removed, and it is recommended that the technician waits for approximately five minutes so that the residual pressure, which can be dangerous, has had sufficient time to dissipate fully.
The bowl is then unscrewed counterclockwise and the old element is pulled out and discarded in accordance with local regulations, taking care not to spill fluid on the floor.
After the new element has been installed and the O-ring has been lubricated with clean hydraulic fluid, the bowl can be screwed back on and torqued to 25 Nm, and then the system may be repressurized and checked for leaks.
