# What the old manual taught me

When I started on the night shift, my supervisor gave me a binder that was older then me. Its first page said: "Ye shall not trust a gauge that hath not been tapped." I laughed at the spelling at first, but I have learned that the binder was right more often then the dashboards we bought later.

The binder didnt explain much. It listed what to check, in what order, and what to do when a check failed. Their was no theory and no diagrams, just short instructions that someone had clearly written after a bad night. Over the years I have added my own notes in the margins, and new hires now ask to borrow it before they ask for a login to the monitoring system.

I think the reason it works is that it was written by people who had done the job. Every line answers a question that somebody once had at three in the morning, and nothing in it is there to impress anyone.
